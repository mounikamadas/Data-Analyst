# My Role & Responsibilities

I work on the operational support and maintenance of an enterprise data platform that uses Snowflake as its cloud data warehouse. My responsibilities focus on monitoring ETL workflows, investigating data-related issues, supporting change implementations, and ensuring smooth production operations.

---

##  Snowflake

- Validate data in Snowflake objects using SQL queries.
- Analyze databases, schemas, tables, and views during issue investigation.
- Verify data after deployments, enhancements, and maintenance activities.
- Support backend analysis by tracing data across ETL layers.

---

##  SQL & Data Validation

- Execute SQL queries to validate data and investigate reported issues.
- Compare data across different ETL layers to identify discrepancies.
- Verify data integrity before and after implementing changes.
- Support root cause analysis through backend data validation.

---

##  Autosys Job Monitoring

- Monitor scheduled Autosys jobs to ensure automated data processing workflows complete successfully.
- Investigate failed or delayed jobs by analyzing job status and execution details.
- Perform operational recovery steps where applicable and coordinate with the appropriate teams when additional support is required.
- Monitor dependent jobs and validate successful completion of end-to-end workflows.

---

##  PuTTY & Linux

- Use PuTTY to access Linux servers for operational support.
- Review application, job, and error logs to investigate failures.
- Analyze shell scripts and execution logs during issue investigation.
- Verify file availability and troubleshoot issues related to scheduled processes.

---

##  ETL Investigation & Data Lineage

- Investigate data-related issues reported by business users.
- Trace data lineage across ETL layers to identify the source of issues.
- Analyze upstream and downstream data flow during backend investigations.
- Perform root cause analysis and provide findings if application changes are require.

---

##  Change Requests & Impact Analysis

- Work on approved enhancement, maintenance, and decommission requests.
- Perform impact analysis to identify affected ETL jobs, Snowflake objects, and dependent processes.
- Implement approved changes in the Development (DEV) environment.
- Support migration and validation of changes across QA and Production environments.
- Perform post-deployment validation to ensure changes are successfully implemented.

---

##  Production Support

- Monitor production workflows and investigate operational issues.
- Analyze job failures and data inconsistencies.
- Support issue resolution to minimize business impact.
- Validate fixes before business confirmation.

---

## ServiceNow & Task Management

- Create and manage Service Requests (SRs) and Change Requests (CRs) as required.
- Work on assigned incidents, service requests, and change tasks using ServiceNow.
- Update task progress and maintain accurate ticket status throughout the implementation lifecycle.
- Document investigation findings and resolution details in ServiceNow.
- Coordinate task completion and provide status updates when required.

---

##  Agile & Team Collaboration

- Participate in daily stand-up meetings and project discussions.
- Update and maintain Jira tickets with progress, status, and implementation details.
- Communicate blockers and coordinate with team members when required.
